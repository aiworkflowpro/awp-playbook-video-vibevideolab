# Topo Glass Explainer (free first chapter): narration

The sample narration, 8 sentences. Ids match `narration.sentences` in the plan. `align.mjs --script` reads one sentence per line (a .txt, or a .tsv with a `text` column); keeping the same number of sentences keeps the timing sentence by sentence.

## Hook

- `n01` Most people use AI the way they would use a very smart reference book.
- `n02` You ask it a question and you get a good answer back in a few seconds.
- `n03` An AI agent is built on a different idea, and that idea is worth understanding properly.
- `n04` You do not ask an agent for an answer, you hand it a whole job to finish.
- `n05` It works out the steps by itself and keeps going until the job is actually done.
- `n06` That sounds like magic until you see how simple the machinery underneath really is.
- `n07` By the end you will know which jobs to hand over and which ones to keep for yourself.

## Title

- `n08` This is how AI agents actually work.
