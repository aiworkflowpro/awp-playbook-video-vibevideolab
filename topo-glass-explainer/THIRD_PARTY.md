# Third-party software and assets

## Remotion

The engine runs on Remotion, installed from the public npm registry under its own licence. Remotion is free for individuals and companies of up to 3 people; a company of 4 or more needs a Remotion Company License (remotion.dev/license). It is not included in this pack.
